<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/lang-en.gif" alt="English" width="800"></p>
<p><span style="font-size:18px;color:#e5e5e5"><strong>RS Polymorph</strong> lets you choose which recipe the Refined Storage Crafting Grid and Pattern Grid use when several recipes share the same ingredients. On every Minecraft version Refined Storage exists on. No Polymorph needed.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:20px;color:#4682c8"><em>Stop letting Refined Storage pick the wrong recipe for you.</em></span></p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/title-features-en.gif" alt="What It Does" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/pick.gif" alt="Two oak planks match three recipes; the recipe button opens a popup, sticks are picked and the grid crafts sticks instead of a ladder" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/icons/button.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:18px"><strong>Recipe button:</strong></span> next to the grid, it lists every recipe that matches your ingredients.</p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/icons/warning.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:18px"><strong>Never miss a conflict:</strong></span> a red warning and a gold halo show when more than one recipe matches.</p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/icons/eye.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:18px"><strong>Live preview:</strong></span> the output changes the moment you pick, and the choice is kept per grid.</p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/pattern.gif" alt="In the Pattern Grid, sticks are picked over the ladder, the pattern is printed and its tooltip reads 4x Stick" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/icons/pattern.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:18px"><strong>Pattern Grid:</strong></span> the same button, and your choice is written into the printed pattern.</p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/icons/gear.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:18px"><strong>The right craft:</strong></span> autocrafting uses the recipe stored on the pattern, not the first match.</p>
<p><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/icons/info.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:18px"><strong>Light on your game:</strong></span> no recipe scan per frame or per shift-crafted item.</p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://team-arcadia.github.io/images/mods/rspolymorph/curseforge/title-start-en.gif" alt="Getting Started" width="800"></h3>
<p><span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:20px"><strong>1.</strong></span> Install Refined Storage and the RS Polymorph jar for your version and loader, client and server.</p>
<p><span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:20px"><strong>2.</strong></span> Open a Crafting Grid or a Pattern Grid and place ingredients.</p>
<p><span style="color:#4682c8;font-size:20px"><strong>3.</strong></span> When the recipe button glows, click it and pick your recipe.</p>
</div>
